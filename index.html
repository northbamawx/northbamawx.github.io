    Fire Danger & Burn Rules

    Whether it's safe and legal to burn outdoors in our counties right now.

    Fire weather alerts

    Checking…

    Alabama summer burn ban

    From May 1 through October 31, the state bans open burning in Madison, Morgan, Lawrence and DeKalb counties to protect air quality.

    Alabama burn permits

    You need a free permit from the Alabama Forestry Commission to burn more than a quarter acre, or any fire within 25 feet of woods or grass. During a drought emergency, all outdoor burning can be banned.

    Current Alabama burn restrictions

    Tennessee burn permits

    From October 15 through May 15, Tennessee requires a free burn permit for outdoor burning. Your city or county may have its own rules.

    Get a Tennessee burn permit

    What the stream does

    Everything on the stream runs on its own. No one has to be awake for the warning to reach you.

    Spoken warnings

    Tornado, severe thunderstorm and flash flood warnings are read aloud the moment they're issued, with the warning outlined on the map.

    Storm mode

    During a warning the stream locks onto the local radar and stays there until the threat is over.

    Hourly updates

    Current conditions, the forecast and the outlook from the Storm Prediction Center, spoken at the top of every hour.

    Tropics

    Hurricane and tropical storm tracking with observed winds, storm surge and damage reports when a storm threatens the Gulf Coast.
