Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

What we collect

NorthBamaWX does not sell, rent or share personal information. The website has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

  • Visitor counts. The website uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that records page views without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Your location. If you tap "Am I in a warning?", your browser shares your location with the page only to check it against active alerts. It never leaves your device and is not stored.
  • Comments and messages. If you comment on or message our YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads or TikTok accounts, our automated assistant may read your message to answer your weather question. We don't keep these messages outside those platforms.

Social media tools

We use the official YouTube, Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) and TikTok developer tools only to post our own weather videos and updates to our own accounts and to reply to comments and messages. We do not collect data about other users through these tools. Each platform's own privacy policy applies to your use of it.

Weather data

Forecasts, alerts and radar come from public sources such as the National Weather Service. Your browser loads some of them directly from those services.
